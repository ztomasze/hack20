# Races

One of the most important aspects of choosing a race is its flavor, cosmetics, culture, and place in the campaign world. See your campaign setting for those details; this section covers only the mechanics.

Ability score improvements granted by your race allow you to exceed the initial +3 starting limit.

Most races include subraces.  While such subraces often include cosmetic physical differences, the more significant differences tend to be cultural. These subraces often require a minimum ability score, representing the teachings or practices that culture tends to foster or instill in its members. You can choose the _Other_ subrace option if your character never adopted the normal culture of their physical subrace, either because they resisted it, were a poor fit for it, or were raised outside of it.

## Human

* **Talented:** Gain +1 to any two different ability scores.
