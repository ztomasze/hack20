# GMing

## Rolling (and Not Rolling) Skills

I usually start with the skill domain.  Might be either/or.

Then name the ability score, which is used if even if you don't have the skill.  Might be an either/or, such as STR or DEX for certain athletic feats or INT or WIS for the application of common sense.

### Knowledge skills

Arcana, History, Nature, and Religion are knowledge skills.

automatic success based on the existence of proficiency
without prof: Hard (DC20)
without expertise: Very Hard (DC20)

don't be afraid to simply say "you don't know this" with no roll if discovering the answer should be part of the adventure, such as casting _identify_, seeking a sage, finding a lost library, etc.

### Perception skills

Insight and Perception are perception skills.

### Social skills

Diplomacy, Fast-Talk, and Intimidation are social skills.

### Movement Modes (Athletics)

Most short athletic tasks that involve movement--such as climbing, leaping, running, or swimming--should offer a choice between using STR or DEX.  While high STR is helpful here, high STR also typically means that your own body is heavier. A halfling or child, with less body mass to move, may be just as capable at athletics using DEX as a heavy warrior who relies on STR.
